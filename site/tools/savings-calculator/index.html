Savings calculator

Estimate what a heat pump, solar panels or a battery would cost and save in your home, and what it would do to your carbon emissions.

What you need

Your postcode and a few details about the house. The estimate gets closer the more it knows:

  • With a postcode and basic details, it works from typical homes like yours.
  • With your home's energy certificate, which it can look up for homes in England, Wales and Scotland, it uses what the assessor recorded.
  • With your actual energy bills, it starts from what you really use.

What it can't tell you

It is an estimate, not a quote or a system design. It can't see your radiators, pipework, roof shading or anything that affects planning. An installer's survey does that.
