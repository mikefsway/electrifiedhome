Watt's House

An ordinary 1930s semi that runs entirely on electricity, shown round by Watt, the cat who lives there. Tap where he should go, upstairs too, and whatever he sits beside is explained in plain English, with its sources.

What you need

Nothing. It works on a computer or a phone, and there's no sound unless you ask for it. Follow Watt on his tour, or send him anywhere in the house. If your device can't show it, or you'd rather read, the same topics are there as a plain list.

What it can't tell you

The house is a typical one, not yours, and its costs are typical ranges, not quotes. It shows what goes where and how the parts work together. What your home needs is decided at an installer's survey.

What next

The 3D house tour is the same house without Watt. For more on each part, read about heat pumps, solar and batteries, cooking, car charging and tariffs.
