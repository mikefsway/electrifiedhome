Tools

Tools that answer questions about your own home: which grants you might get, what a heat pump would cost to run, what one sounds like and whether it fits. Most work from your postcode or your phone. None needs an account.

  • Savings calculator

    Estimate what a heat pump, solar panels or a battery would cost and save in your home.

    Needs your postcode

  • Grant finder

    Your postcode and four questions show which UK grants you might get.

    Needs your postcode

  • Boiler escape plan

    Work out when to replace a working gas, oil or LPG boiler, and what to do before then.

    Needs your boiler's age

  • Installer finder

    Heat pump, solar and battery installers near you, each with a link to the MCS register.

    Needs a postcode

  • Heat pump visualiser

    See a true-size heat pump or hot water cylinder where you'd put it.

    Needs a phone camera

  • Sound simulator

    Play a real heat pump recording and measure it from your windows and garden.

    Needs a phone and a speaker

  • 3D house tour

    A cutaway house showing every part of a heat pump system, with solar panels and a battery.

    Needs nothing

Where they come from

The tools are made by KarbonKit. Each page here embeds one exactly as any other website would. If you run a website for a council, installer or community group, you can put them on it for free.
