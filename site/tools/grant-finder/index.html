Grant finder

Your postcode and four questions show which grants and funding schemes you might get for a heat pump, insulation or solar panels.

What you need

Your postcode, whether you own or rent, how the home is heated now, its EPC rating if you know it, and whether the household gets a means-tested benefit or has a lower income. It takes a couple of minutes.

What it can't tell you

The scheme itself decides whether you qualify, often after a survey. Each result links to the official page, and shows when it was last checked against it. Councils sometimes run their own schemes that may not be listed.

What next

Follow the official links to apply. For the Boiler Upgrade Scheme your installer applies for you: see choosing an installer. More on the schemes on paying for it.
