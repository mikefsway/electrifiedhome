Heat pump visualiser

See a true-size heat pump in your garden, or a hot water cylinder in your cupboard, through your phone's camera.

What you need

A phone: Safari on an iPhone or Chrome on Android. There's nothing to install. On a computer you get a 3D model you can turn around instead.

The models are drawn to the measurements of common heat pumps of different sizes, and a 200 litre cylinder. They can't be scaled, so what you see is the real size.

What it can't tell you

The unit your installer specifies may be a different size, and it needs space around it for air to flow. Ask for the clearances.

What next

Hear what one sounds like with the sound simulator, or read more about heat pumps.
