Installer finder

Find heat pump, solar panel and battery installers near you.

What you need

A postcode. You can filter by heat pumps, solar panels or batteries.

What it can't tell you

The list is built from public records such as Companies House and installers' own websites. Nobody has vetted it, and each result says where its technology information came from. Check an installer on the MCS register before you get in touch.
