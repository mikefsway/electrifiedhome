Tools

3D house tour

An ordinary 1930s semi that runs entirely on electricity, cut open so you can see how the parts fit together: the heat pump, hot water cylinder, radiators and controls, solar panels, a home battery, the car charger and an induction hob. Each is explained in plain English, with its sources.

What you need

Nothing. It works on a computer or a phone, and the 3D house only loads when you press start. Take the guided tour, or pick a room and look around. If your device can't show it, or you'd rather read, the same topics are there as a plain list.

Needs your postcode

The tour shows a typical house, and its costs are typical ranges, not quotes. Where things go in yours is decided at the installer's survey. For more on each part, read about heat pumps, solar and batteries, cooking, car charging and tariffs.
