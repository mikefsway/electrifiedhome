Tools

Plan it with your own AI assistant

If you already use an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, it can take you through the boiler escape plan as a conversation: when to replace your boiler, how ready your home is for a heat pump, and the grants you could get.

What you need

An AI assistant you can type into, and about ten minutes. Copy the message below into a new chat and answer its questions. It doesn't need your name, and it only asks for your postcode if you want it to look up your energy certificate.

What it can't tell you

The assistant takes its figures from the same tools as the boiler escape plan, but it can still word things wrongly. If a figure matters to you, check it in the boiler escape plan itself. Neither can say what your home needs: an installer's survey does that.

Who sees what

What you type goes to your assistant's company, under your own settings there. Electrified Home doesn't see the conversation, and KarbonKit doesn't keep the answers its tools are sent.

What next

If you'd rather not use an assistant, the boiler escape plan covers the same ground. Either way, choosing an installer is the next step.
