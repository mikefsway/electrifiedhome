Tools

Home energy helper

It takes you through the checks on this site one question at a time – what a heat pump, solar panels or a battery would cost and save, a plan for replacing your boiler, your radiators and grants – and carries your answers from one check to the next.

What you need

A few minutes for each check. Some ask for your postcode, and some can look up your home's energy performance certificate if you give them your postcode and house number. None of them ask for your name or email.

Three ways to answer

  • Tap through the questions here, choosing from the answers each one offers.
  • Choose "Chat instead" to type your answers. An AI writes the replies, one question at a time, and takes its figures from the same checks.
  • Choose "Use your own AI assistant instead" if you already use ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant. Copy the message it gives you into a new chat there, and your assistant asks the questions. You can read the message before you paste it.

All three ask the same questions and give the same figures.

Your logbook

When you finish a check, the helper gives you a short message that holds your answers. Keep it, and you can carry on later here or in your own assistant without starting again. "Open your logbook" shows what you have so far, and can remember it on this device.

What it can't tell you

The figures are estimates from your answers and, if you look it up, your energy certificate. In the chat or your own assistant an AI writes the words, and it can still word things wrongly. If a figure matters to you, check it in the tool itself: the savings calculator, boiler escape plan, radiator check or grant finder. None of them can say what your home needs: an installer's survey does that.

Who sees what

When you tap through the questions, your answers go to KarbonKit, which makes the helper, to work out the figures, and aren't kept. In the chat, what you type goes to KarbonKit and to Anthropic, whose AI writes the replies. The chat's note "What happens to what I type" says what each of them keeps. With your own assistant, what you type goes to that assistant's company, under your own settings there. Electrified Home doesn't see your answers or your conversation either way. The about page has more.

What next

When you know what you want, choosing an installer is the next step. The helper can point you to installers near you.
