About this site

Electrified Home explains how to run a UK home on electricity, and has tools you can try on your own home.

Who runs it

The site is run by the team that makes KarbonKit, a set of free tools that councils, installers and community energy groups put on their own websites. This site embeds them the same way those websites do, and KarbonKit uses it to test them. Nobody pays to appear here, and the site sells nothing.

Accuracy

The pages are general information for UK homes, not advice for yours. They avoid prices and grant amounts because those change often. The tools carry those numbers instead. The grant finder shows when each scheme was last checked against its official page, and the calculator is updated for each energy price cap.

If you spot something wrong, email hello@karbonkit.com.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising.

The tools load from widgets.karbonkit.com. What you type into them, such as a postcode, goes to KarbonKit to work out the answer. The tools set no cookies either. If you ask the boiler escape plan to email you a plan, your address is used to send it. KarbonKit's privacy page has the details.
